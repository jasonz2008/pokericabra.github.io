Delete Your Data

App: Pokericabra Trainee  ·  Developer: RamPublish

This page explains how to request deletion of data associated with Pokericabra Trainee, published by RamPublish.

Pokericabra Trainee has no user accounts and no servers of its own. RamPublish operates no database and holds no personal data about players. Almost everything the game stores is on your own device, and the optional cloud save is stored in your own Google Account. Most deletion is therefore immediate and entirely under your control — the steps below tell you exactly where.

How to request deletion

  1. To delete everything stored on your device — settings, progress, local high scores, gold balance and any in-progress run:
    Uninstall the app, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Pokericabra Trainee → Storage → Clear storage. This is immediate and complete.
  2. To delete your cloud save (only exists if you signed in to Google Play Games):
    Open the Google Play Games app → Settings → Delete Play Games account data, and choose this game. You can also revoke the game's access to your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  3. To delete advertising data held by Google:
    Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID.
  4. To ask us to delete anything else, or if you want written confirmation, email support@pokericabra.com with the subject “Data deletion request”. We respond within 30 days. Please tell us which app you are writing about; we may ask for information to verify the request, and if we cannot verify it we will tell you why.

What is deleted, what is kept

DataHeld byWhat happens
Settings, game progress, local high scores, gold, upgrade levels, in-progress run Your device only Deleted immediately on uninstall or Clear storage. We never receive it.
Cloud save (gold balance, lifetime gold, upgrade levels) Your own Google Account (Drive app-data) Deleted when you delete it using step 2. RamPublish cannot read, export or delete any player's save.
Advertising identifier, IP address, ad interaction data Google (AdMob) Governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Google's retention practices. RamPublish holds none of it, and receives only aggregate, non-identifying revenue reporting.
Purchase and subscription records Google Play Kept by Google Play under your Google Account. Tax and accounting records are retained as long as the law requires, typically 6–10 years. These cannot be deleted on request.
Support emails you send us RamPublish Deleted on request, and in any case within 24 months.

Additional retention

Beyond the purchase and tax records noted above, RamPublish applies no additional retention period: there is no account to close, no profile to archive and no backup of player data on any system we control.
