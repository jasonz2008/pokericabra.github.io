1. Summary — the short version

This summary highlights the main processing activities. The detail follows, and the detail governs.

  • Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing and handled on your device. Local delivery records help prevent repeated rewards. This version does not send purchases to a developer-operated verification server.
  • Almost everything the App stores stays on your device, in the App's private storage, and is deleted when you uninstall.
  • Third-party services process data: Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network and Google Mobile Ads for advertising; Google UMP for advertising privacy; Google Analytics for Firebase for optional usage and revenue measurement; Google Play Billing for purchases; and Google Play Games Services for optional cloud saves and leaderboards.
  • We do not sell your personal information. Serving personalised advertising may count as "sharing" or a "sale" under some US state laws; you can turn it off — see Section 9.
  • The App is not for children. Minimum age 13, or 16 in the EEA and wherever local law sets a higher age.
  • You control optional advertising choices and Play Games participation. Non-personalised advertising and purchase processing can still involve necessary processing. Uninstalling stops future app requests but does not automatically delete records held by Google Play or other providers.

3.2 Advertising and privacy choices

The App uses Unity LevelPlay mediation with Google Mobile Ads, Unity Ads and Meta Audience Network for banner, interstitial and optional rewarded advertisements. Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) manages advertising privacy choices. The active ad sources depend on the published LevelPlay configuration.

Advertising providers may process advertising/app identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, device and technical information, advertising interactions and diagnostic/fraud-prevention information. This information is processed by the providers through their SDKs. RamPublish can access aggregate advertising and revenue reports.

UMP requests updated consent information at launch. Ads wait for UMP to permit advertising requests; an error or timeout does not itself grant permission. Completing the form does not imply agreement to all vendors or personalised advertising. LevelPlay reads UMP's vendor consent and Additional Consent information. US sale/sharing/targeted-advertising opt-outs and Global Privacy Control are decoded from UMP's GPP data and passed to LevelPlay's do-not-sell setting. Where required, Ad Privacy Settings lets you reopen the privacy form and change your choices. Existing ad caches are discarded when changing choices. Declining optional advertising choices does not prevent gameplay.

Provider information: Google privacy, Unity privacy, and Meta privacy.

3.3 Purchases

The App offers a one-month prepaid, non-renewing ad-free subscription (remove_ads_30d), a consumable gold/potion pack and non-consumable card-border cosmetics. Google Play Billing processes payments; we never receive payment-card information.

The App obtains product details and purchase status through Google Play Billing. Completed purchases are processed on your device. Pending payments do not grant items. Delivery is saved before the App acknowledges or consumes the purchase; failed processing can be retried through Play.

The App stores purchase-token hashes, local delivery/bonus markers and cached entitlements in its private device storage. Earlier releases may have stored raw tokens locally for delivery recovery. Purchase tokens are not intentionally written to application logs or sent to a developer-operated server in this version. Google retains transaction records under its own policies. RamPublish can access purchase/order information available in Google Play Console for revenue reporting and support.

Local records support delivery, duplicate prevention and restoration. Subscription expiry and refunded ownership are refreshed through Play queries at launch, on resume and during long sessions. Offline cached ad-free access does not itself unlock subscriber rewards. Contact support@pokericabra.com with your order reference for purchase support; do not email payment-card details or raw purchase tokens.

Google's processing is described in Google's Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms.

3.4 Optional cloud save and leaderboards — Google Play Games Services

If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games Services, the App stores a single saved-game snapshot in your own Google Drive app-data storage, under your Google Account.

  • What is stored: gold and lifetime progression, upgrades, earned cosmetics, daily rewards, stamina, statistics, tasks, collections, tutorial progress and consumable inventory. Paid cosmetic ownership is checked through Google Play Billing.
  • What is not stored: your high scores, your ad-free entitlement, any device identifier, any advertising identifier, and any free-text you have entered.
  • Where it is stored: in your Google Account, managed by Google. We cannot access it. We have no console, no export and no ability to read any player's save.
  • Sign-in is optional. The App asks at most once per installation. If you decline, the game works exactly as before on your local save, and we do not ask again.
  • How to remove it: delete the game's saved data from your Google Play Games account, or revoke the App's access in your Google Account settings.

Google's processing of your Google Account and Play Games data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Leaderboard submissions send your score and encoded run/category details to Google Play Games. Public rankings can display your Play Games display name, avatar, score and rank. These are separate from the saved-game snapshot.

3.5 Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to put in the message. We use it only to answer you, and delete the correspondence within 24 months.

3.6 Optional usage and revenue measurement

The App integrates Google Analytics for Firebase to measure installations, sessions, device/app characteristics, advertising impressions and their reported revenue, and supported in-app purchase events. Analytics can use app-instance and advertising identifiers where permitted. We do not send player names, email addresses, full game saves, payment-card details or raw purchase tokens as custom Analytics event parameters.

The dedicated Pokericabra Analytics property shares measurement events and revenue with our linked Google Ads account to assess acquisition costs and return on advertising spend. Personalized advertising through that account link is disabled. Analytics reports are separate from the final earnings and transaction statements supplied by advertising networks and Google Play.

Collection starts only after the App's policy agreement and an applicable UMP Analytics consent signal. An unknown, unconfigured or refused Analytics choice does not enable collection. Permission to show an advertisement is not itself Analytics consent. Where UMP indicates the purpose does not require consent, measurement can operate subject to applicable law and the opt-out below.

You can turn off future Analytics collection in Settings → Analytics Settings. This also resets the device's local Analytics data; it does not erase data already received by Google. Where available, Ad Privacy Settings also lets you change the consent purposes. Google's processing is described in Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information.

3.7 What the App does not do

For the avoidance of doubt, and stated positively because template privacy policies routinely over-claim:

  • The App contains no crash-reporting SDK. Optional usage, revenue and install measurement is described above.
  • The App has no user accounts, no login, no username and no profile operated by us.
  • The App does not request or access your precise GPS location, contacts, calendar, camera, microphone, photos, files, SMS, call logs, health data or biometrics.
  • The App does not send full gameplay histories or game saves to Analytics. The SDK's usage and session measurement is described above.
  • This version does not send purchase records or full game saves to a developer-operated server.

11. Security

Purchases use Google Play Billing. New local delivery markers use hashed purchase tokens and are saved together with inventory before acknowledgement or consumption. On-device data uses the App's private storage and operating-system application isolation. Advertising and platform SDKs use their providers' network services. These measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security.

No system is perfectly secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, and you acknowledge that you provide data at your own risk. Data stored on your device may be accessible if your device is rooted, jailbroken, lost, or compromised by malware — please protect your device with a screen lock and keep its software updated.
