Scope: `/drawply/`, its installation and legal pages, and `drawply/style.css`. This preserves the established independent product identity after the root portfolio redesign. The root page's embedded frame may use the portfolio's surroundings; the annotation runtime remains its own surface.

Creative north star: **Digital studio critique sheet**. Pale-blue paper, graphite text, cobalt controls and a vermilion editorial underline frame practical annotation work.

The source tokens remain `--ink: #172848`, `--muted: #53617b`, `--blue: #184beb`, `--line: #ccd6e8`, `--paper: #fff`, `--wash: #ecf1ff`, and `--red: #d53936`. Cobalt identifies actions and product emphasis; vermilion identifies the editorial mark and visible keyboard focus. Do not inherit the portfolio's charcoal-and-brass skin here.

Self-hosted Manrope carries the wordmark and major headings; Segoe UI and Arial carry practical body text and controls. Hero headings are heavy and tightly tracked. The 1280px container has 40px desktop gutters, 24px below 1000px, 20px below 760px, and 16px below 380px. The hero's unequal copy/demo columns stack on mobile. The three-image result sequence preserves its reading order and complete image aspect ratios.

The live annotation frame uses a white surface, 16px corners, a fine border and the diffuse `0 20px 50px -24px #32477666` shadow. Surrounding reading areas stay flat. Its title and footer explain the working sample; marks remain local to the demo. Height follows validated messages from the active embedded window, with a mobile source at 760px. Preserve this content-aware sizing.

The download section uses cobalt as a full field with a white action and a darker installation panel. Code is readable and wraps; details and copy actions retain their native behavior. Legal pages narrow the reading measure. Smooth scrolling becomes immediate for reduced motion.
